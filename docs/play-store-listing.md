## Full description (4000)
Credabilia is the memorabilia marketplace where every piece comes with context. Discover signed sports memorabilia, trading cards, comics, art and more, and see exactly how much evidence stands behind each item before you buy.

SEE THE EVIDENCE
Every listing shows an Item Credibility score out of 100. It blends the issuer's own rating (80%) with the community's audits (20%), so you can see where the confidence comes from. Certificate details, issuer lookups and photos are all in one place.

MEET KING CREDION
King Credion is Credabilia's AI guide. Add a signature close-up and he gives a plain-language first impression of the strokes and, as his library of verified signatures grows, compares it with examples of the same signer. His opinion is a helpful second look, never a guarantee of authenticity, and he always says so.

LOOK CLOSER TOGETHER
Join the community audit queue: review the photos and the story, give a quick take, and explain your reasoning. Every audit helps other collectors decide.

SELL IN SECONDS
Snap one photo and AI drafts the title, description, category and even a signature close-up for you. Backgrounds are cleaned up automatically. Choose auction or a fixed price, offer free shipping, and track every sale from one place.

BUY WITH CONFIDENCE
Payments run through Stripe and are held until you have received and checked your item. Shipping labels, tracking and delivery updates are built in. If something is wrong, you can report a problem before the seller is paid.

MEET IN PERSON, SAFELY
Prefer local pickup? Items can be handed off at a police-station safe-exchange spot near you, with a handoff code so both sides know the exchange is complete.

MESSAGES, ALERTS AND MORE
Ask a seller a question, arrange a meetup, and get notified about bids, orders and shipping. Rate sales and read real reviews from other collectors.

Sign in with Google or an email code. Delete your account any time from Profile and settings.
